## 07. Four gaps. Four practical fixes.

Chapter: Research

Research 

### Four gaps. Four practical fixes.

Capture useful details, give clear directions, improve search information and align the facts.

01 

### Capture the details that matter

The source audit found the main contact form’s phone field optional; the admissions landing form already requires it.

Proposed fix  Require a parent phone number; capture grade, city and preferred visit date.

02 

### Make the campus easy to find

The checked contact-page map showed the general Bibinagar area.

Proposed fix  Use the verified school entrance, exact map pin and arrival directions.

03 

### Give each page a clear search purpose

The audit reports 404 responses for standard sitemap and robots URLs, plus generic repeated metadata.

Proposed fix  Publish a valid sitemap and specific page titles and descriptions.

04 

### Give parents one consistent set of facts

Faculty and court figures varied across pages. Fees and travel details were not found in the checked journey.

Proposed fix  Approve a current school fact sheet, plus useful boarding, fee and visit information.

Findings from the supplied research, not a new audit. Missing sitemap files alone do not mean Google cannot index the school.

## 75. Make every projection traceable.

Chapter: Execution

Execution 

### Make every projection traceable.

Budget and conversion assumptions determine the forecast.

Advertising budget ÷ Cost per enquiry × Qualification rate = Qualified enquiries

Qualified enquiries × Booking rate × Attendance rate = Attended visits

Attended visits × Admission rate = Admissions

### Use a reasonable basis.

Agree evidence for enquiry cost, qualification, booking, attendance and admission rates before estimating results.

### Keep the metrics distinct.

Report qualified enquiries, attended visits and admissions separately. Media cost excludes service, production and other costs.

If no campus visit is attributed, media cost per attended visit is not yet calculable.
